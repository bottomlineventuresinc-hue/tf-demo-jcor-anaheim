Asked every week

Straight answers about mess, timings and money.

Will there be mess?

Yes. Doorways are sealed with zipped dust screens when we work indoors, floor protection goes on every route through the house, a dumpster sits on the drive not the lawn, and there is a sweep-down at the end of every working day.

Will it run over?

Most jobs finish on the written programme. Weather, hidden damage, and made-to-order delays are the honest exceptions. You hear from your site lead within 24 hours with the revised date in writing.

How do payments work?

Staged in the contract: 10% to book, 30% on start, 30% at mid-point, 25% at second fix, and the final 5% only after the snag list is cleared at sign-off.

Do we have to move out?

Usually not. Plumbing and electrical remodels are staged so one bath or kitchen path stays usable when the layout allows.
